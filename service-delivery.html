Service Delivery Policy

BookMyTestIreland provides a digital booking-assistance service. No physical goods are shipped or delivered.

How the service begins

The service begins after payment and the required booking preferences are received, subject to the final commencement definition: [BUSINESS OWNER TO CONFIRM WHEN SERVICE WORK IS DEEMED TO BEGIN].

Communication method

We communicate through the email, phone, WhatsApp or other contact method provided by the customer.

How confirmation is delivered

When a suitable appointment is secured, confirmation details are sent digitally using the contact information supplied by the customer.

Typical process

The customer provides centre and date preferences, pays the service fee, and BookMyTestIreland assists with searching for a suitable appointment. Timing depends on RSA appointment availability and the flexibility of the customer preferences.

Factors affecting delivery time

Delivery time may be affected by RSA availability, customer eligibility, preferred centre demand, acceptable date range, alternative centres and how quickly the customer responds to requests for information.

Third-party availability dependency

RSA controls appointment availability and eligibility. BookMyTestIreland cannot promise an exact completion time, date or centre.

BookMyTestIreland is independent from RSA and is operated by AJAY ANIL SREE.
