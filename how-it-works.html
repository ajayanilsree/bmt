How It Works

We assist with searching for a suitable driving-test appointment based on your agreed RSA centre and date preferences.

1

Submit preferences

Share your preferred RSA test centre, alternative centres, acceptable date range, preferred time and notes.

2

Pay the fee

Pay the €40 BookMyTestIreland service fee when the Razorpay payment link is active.

3

Search assistance

We assist with searching for a suitable appointment according to the preferences agreed with you.

4

Booking details

Once a suitable appointment is successfully secured, we send the relevant booking details to you.

Appointment availability is controlled by RSA. BookMyTestIreland cannot guarantee a particular date, time or test centre.
