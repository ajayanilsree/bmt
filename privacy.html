Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

BookMyTestIreland is operated by AJAY ANIL SREE. This policy explains how we handle information for the independent booking-assistance service.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, preferred test centre, alternative centres, date and time preferences, booking notes, payment status information and service communications.

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, provide booking assistance, process service communications, support payment-provider review, maintain records and comply with applicable obligations.

Payment processing

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider such as Razorpay. BookMyTestIreland does not ask you to enter card details directly into this website and does not directly store payment-card numbers.

Third-party processors

We may use third-party services for payment processing, hosting, email, forms, messaging or security. These providers process information only as needed for the relevant service.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, handle support queries, keep transaction records and meet legal or operational requirements. Final retention periods should be confirmed before production launch.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including using HTTPS when deployed and avoiding direct storage of card information. No online service is completely secure.
