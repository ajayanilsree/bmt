Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about the service, availability, pricing and RSA independence.

What does BookMyTestIreland do?

We provide independent driving-test appointment booking assistance for customers in Ireland based on their preferred centres, alternative centres, date range and booking preferences.

Are you part of RSA?

No. BookMyTestIreland is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Road Safety Authority.

Can you guarantee a specific test centre?

No. We can prioritise your preferred centre, but availability is controlled by RSA.

Can you guarantee a particular date?

No. Appointment dates depend on RSA availability and can disappear quickly.

How much does the service cost?

The BookMyTestIreland service fee is €40.

What happens if my first-choice centre is unavailable?

We may consider the alternative centres you have agreed to accept, subject to availability and your date or time preferences.

How quickly can an appointment be secured?

There is no exact timeframe. Timing depends on RSA appointment availability, your preferences and how flexible your alternatives are.

Do I still need to meet RSA eligibility requirements?

Yes. RSA eligibility requirements and terms continue to apply. We do not control eligibility decisions.

Is the €40 fee the RSA driving-test fee?

No. The €40 fee is the BookMyTestIreland service fee and is separate from any fee payable directly to RSA.

How will I know when my appointment has been booked?

We will contact you using the communication details you provide and share confirmation once a suitable appointment has been secured.

What information will I need to provide?

You will need to provide contact details, preferred test centre, acceptable alternatives, date range, preferred time and any relevant notes. Additional RSA-related information may be needed before a booking can be completed.

What happens if I change my mind?

Please contact us as soon as possible. Refund eligibility depends on whether work has begun and the policy conditions stated in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

What is your refund policy?

The refund policy is set out on the Refund & Cancellation Policy page. Some business-owner decisions are intentionally marked as placeholders before launch.
